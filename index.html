            How to play

            Pick the artist. Take turns. On your turn an 80s hit plays and you pick the artist from four choices. Each right answer scores a point. Most points after the last round wins.

            Shout it out. A song plays and everyone shouts out the artist. Tap reveal, then tap everyone who got it right. Each right answer scores a point.

            Music. Each song plays as a 30-second clip with the artist and title hidden until the guesses are in. No account needed, just an internet connection.
